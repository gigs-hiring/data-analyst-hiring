Hi everyone. 👋 I would like to share an update on the usage pattern and retention analysis. 📈

## TLDR 
* After a significant drop a few months ago, ACME Phone users with unlimited plans are once again increasing their average monthly usage, pushing towards 8GB. 🚀
* The 2 new projects have started strong in terms of subscriber numbers and data usage. 🚀
* We should ensure network capacity from our network partners, as especially People Mobile is increasing in both number of subscribers and average monthly usage (>20GB/month). 
* The retention rate of the 2 new projects is lower than the older project’s unfortunately. 📉


## Limited Allowance Plans usage patterns

Users on 1GB and 5GB allowance consume mostly stable amounts of data throughout their lifetime.
* 1GB: 300-400 MB monthly
* 5GB: 1.5-2.0 GB monthly

## Unlimited Data Plans usage patterns - ACME Phone

As our oldest partner, ACME phone users have provided a lot of data in the past 12+ months. 
ACME Phone users with unlimited plans:
* initially started strong last year with more than 10GB of average monthly usage
* then dropped for a few months at ±6GB
* in the past couple of months have increased closer to 8GB.
In the next few months we will observe whether there is a seasonality effect (e.g. strong data usage in spring and summer, that will again drop in the autumn)

## People Mobile

Very strong start at >20GB of average monthly usage for the unlimited users. As we collect more data, in the next few months we will observe their usage patterns further.

## SmartDevices Inc.

Our wearables partner has a lower average monthly usage than the smartphone users. Still so far they use a non-neglectible average ±3GB monthly on the unlimited plan.

## New Projects Retention

The 2 new projects show lower Retention Rate than ACME Phone did at the same point in time..
* People Mobile 85% after 60 days
